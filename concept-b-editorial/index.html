Serina links existing drugs to a synthetic polymer designed to control how much drug is attached and how fast it is released. SER-252 is investigational, not FDA-approved.

Ordinary dosing gives peaks and troughs. Serina’s design goal is exposure that stays in range after a long-acting injection. A schematic after the company’s own diagram.
