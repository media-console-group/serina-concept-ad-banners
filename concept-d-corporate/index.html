Introducing SER-252 (POZ-Apomorphine)

Apomorphine attached to POZ, in a Phase 1b study (mainly safety, tolerability and drug levels) in advanced Parkinson’s disease. Investigational and not FDA-approved.

  • Cohort 1: eight patients, lowest dose, randomized 3:1 to SER-252 or placebo.
  • Sep 9, 2026, per the company: an independent committee reviewed blinded safety data and recommended Cohort 2. Not proof SER-252 is safe or works.
  • Blinded, exploratory, preliminary: may not be evidence of treatment effect.
  • Company expects topline data from the single-dose part in H1 2027; timing may change.
  • Company announced an FDA clinical hold (Nov 3, 2025), then that FDA cleared the IND (Jan 28, 2026).
